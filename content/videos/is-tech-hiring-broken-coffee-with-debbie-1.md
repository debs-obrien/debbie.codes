I wanted a little show to get some thoughts out there, connect with people, and maybe help some people along the way. I've been mentoring a couple of women lately and I'm seeing a lot of the same things coming up, so this felt like the best way I can help more people. I don't even know why I'm calling it Coffee with Debbie, because I don't even drink coffee.

Today I talk about hiring, and how hiring is broken, because I'm going through the process myself. This is not a scary video. I'm being pretty particular about where I want to work, and I don't want anyone to take my path as the way the industry is for everyone.

I get into DevRel roles that want you living out of a suitcase, why so many jobs are still centered on San Francisco (it kind of feels like the village pub again), hybrid and the remote question, what long interview loops take out of you, how AI is shrinking teams, how I actually apply for jobs with AI, recruiters and empathy, and why we need to bring the human touch back.
