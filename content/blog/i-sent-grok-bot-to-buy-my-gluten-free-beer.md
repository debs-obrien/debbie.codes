The home bot had the school-bag list for the twins. There was also an Amazon restock that already went through. Some of this is already running. I am not starting from a blank page in this video.

I want the bot to shop because this is the boring stuff that still has to get done. Compare shops. Build a cart. Book a slot. If it can do that, I can keep adding to the cart instead of starting over every time I remember something.
