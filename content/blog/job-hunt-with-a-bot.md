Hi everyone. Unfortunately I am now in a position where I need to look for a job. I don't want to go into the whys. I just want to show you how I'm searching in times like this.

I created a job hunt bot on Grok Bot. Literally just named it job hunt. It came back ready for roles, companies, applications, the whole pipeline. I said: take a look at me online, find all the details you know about me, and help me find my next role. Ask me any questions you need.

If you don't have a public profile like me, just give it something to work from. LinkedIn. A bit about yourself. Whatever. Because from what I gave it, look what it came back with.

It pulled my public trail together. Palma based. Teacher, builder. Playwright community at Microsoft. A short applied AI DevRel stint. Now platform engineer at Zephyr Cloud. The through line is teaching people to build with Playwright, MCP and agents while actually shipping the workflows myself.

And then this bit was insane. Biggest public signal: in March I called Anthropic's developer education lead my ideal role, except the office / remote bit looks like a hard constraint with the kids. It found that from March. From something I tweeted or posted on LinkedIn. And it said, okay, this is the kind of role you want. Incredible.

I'm about four months into Zephyr after two layoffs in a row. I've had really bad luck this year. So this is really something.

## Fresh search this morning

This morning I asked the bot again from my phone. Any other jobs out there? I'm showing you on desktop here. Fresh pass for anything new since Friday. Cursor and Grok Bot first, then other AI agent and education roles.

Nothing new overnight on those boards. Same as Friday. The wider AI search was still running. Wider passes for Spain remote are still thin. The new agent roles are mostly US offices. So those only matter if I'd ask for an exception.

Closest to what I want: Anthropic technical enablement lead for the Claude platform. Anthropic DevRel for Claude Code and Claude. Databricks. Diagrid DevRel. Things I can hover over and read without even clicking, just to see if they're of interest. And you can see where it's getting the information from. Different websites. Not all the same place. It's going through them for me so I don't have to.

It even gives an opinion. If I were picking one exception to test, try Anthropic enablement or Databricks. Want me to prep either, or keep watching Cursor?

I can say tell me more about the Databricks one. It pulls the information. Pulls the full JD. Walks me through it. Interview prep. The lot.

## Desktop, mobile, leave it running

You're on desktop. You're on mobile. You can walk away. Come back. Leave it running in the background. Give it a workflow so it searches every day and comes back with new ones.

I have no idea if this is going to be a good way for me to actually find a job. But it's a really nice experience. It helps me discover what's out there in a much easier way.

I'll keep posting on X about how I'm doing. Let's see if I get a job very soon.
